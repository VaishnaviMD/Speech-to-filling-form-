# FormSathi AI: Demo Shot List

**Setup before recording:** Chrome or Edge, laptop at 1280×800 (or a phone screen recording), sound on, a quiet room. Language set to **हिंदी** or **English**. Run through it once first so mic permission is already granted. Have the filled PDF viewer ready to open.

| # | Time | Shot | Voice-over / on-screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:12 | **Hook.** A photo of a printed government form (or hands holding one), then a slow zoom on "IFSC", "DBT" and "Annual family income". | "My grandmother can tell you her whole life story, but she can't fill in her own pension form. It's in English and full of words like IFSC and DBT. So she pays a stranger and hands over her Aadhaar." |
| 2 | 0:12–0:18 | Title card: **FormSathi AI. Fill any form just by talking.** | "So I built FormSathi AI." |
| 3 | 0:18–0:26 | Home screen → tap **वृद्धावस्था पेंशन फ़ॉर्म** (or upload a PDF). Show the 3 Mode Switcher tabs: Visual, Voice-Assisted, and Conversational. | "Upload any PDF form. FormSathi AI finds every field and turns it into simple spoken questions in your language." |
| 4 | 0:26–0:50 | **Conversational Voice Mode.** Assistant asks question. Speaker answers: "My name is Vaishnavi". FormSathi strips the conversational wrapper, fills "Vaishnavi", and spells it out letter-by-letter for confirmation. | Let the app's voice play: "She answers in her language. Letters and digits are verified aloud with zero tapping needed." |
| 5 | 0:50–1:02 | **Jargon explainer.** At the IFSC field, tap **❓ इसका मतलब क्या है?** The explanation appears and is spoken. | "Don't know a word? Just ask." |
| 6 | 1:02–1:10 | **Organisation Form Builder & QR Code.** Show the custom form builder, generating a printable QR code for physical desks. | "Hospitals and colleges can create custom forms and print QR codes in seconds." |
| 7 | 1:10–1:20 | Fast-forward to 🎉 screen → **AI Evaluation Score 100%** → **Download filled PDF** → open the PDF with every field filled. | "AI evaluates every response before downloading the filled form, ready to print." |
| 8 | 1:20–1:30 | End card: FormSathi AI — Multilingual, Accessible, Zero Retention. | "Open models on Groq, fully zero-retention in browser memory. FormSathi AI bridges the digital divide." |

**Backup plan:** if the network is slow on recording day, use the sample form (it needs no extraction call) and type one answer to show the typed fallback.

**Cut-down to 60 s:** drop shot 6 and shorten shot 4 to two fields.
